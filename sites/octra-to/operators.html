BUILD A BETTER WAY THROUGH

Run a route.
Own the terms.

An operator can welcome new devices through a free Circle-hosted front door, then sell finite byte allowances at its own published OCT rates. The protocol takes zero commission.

01 / THE FRONT DOOR

Help people in.

Publish a small, exact allowlist of public Circle assets for instructions and offer discovery. The registration-only route cannot carry arbitrary Circle or Internet traffic.

02 / THE PAID ROUTE

Sell finite bytes.

Post a public tariff with explicit traffic classes. A user accepts a plan and signs a purchase. The node waits for canonical confirmation before it forwards those bytes.

03 / THE SETTLEMENT

Keep what you earn.

OCT moves between payer and operator. Available channel value can include earned OCT, so an exit operator can fund its own usage without first moving earnings out.

THE RULES ARE VISIBLE

Price freely.
Change carefully.

The current devnet payment Circle delays a new rate for 24 hours, giving payers time to leave. A payer can withdraw available channel OCT immediately; settled purchases stay final. An operator’s stake has a seven-day unstake period. Service still depends on a reachable control plane, canonical state and working routes.
